## Processing and locality

The 2026-11-21 expansion replaces the earlier Serengeti elephant soundscape
with a zoo call and two research recordings from wild elephants. The National
Park Service MP3s and Freesound previews retain their source audio, except for
the capuchin clip, which was boosted 8 dB and re-encoded. The cougar audio was
extracted from camera-trap video and boosted 23 dB. The elephant rumbles and
Yesal howler were attenuated by 3 and 2 dB respectively for peak headroom and
encoded as MP3. The Pinbury fox was converted to MP3 and remains CC BY-SA 4.0.
Playback speed and the original call sequences were preserved.

The Manyara track runs 1:52 after trimming eight seconds of quiet lead-in and
reducing the gain by 4 dB. The 16-second growl sequence uses the unmodified
Freesound MP3 preview. Its map point represents Tanzania because the recordist
did not specify a site.

Identification follows the recordists. Fox, elephant, and howler monkey remain
broad labels because the source does not establish an exact species. The tiger
is captive at Louisville Zoo; the lion was recorded at Ukutula Game Reserve,
with managed/captive status unresolved. These are shown at their recording
sites, never relocated to a species’ native range. Other source context is
retained in the catalog and shown in the status information.

The new files passed full decode and signal checks. Source descriptions supply
the identification and call context. Published landmark or reserve locations
are used where exact microphone coordinates are unavailable. The leopard
cough lasts less than half a second and plays once at its original length.

Published Freesound map centers are approximate locations. The tiger source
names Louisville Zoo but has no geotag; its approximate site uses the
[zoo’s official event calendar coordinates](https://louisvillezoo.org/events/category/events/2026-06/?outlook-ical=1).

The National Park Service and MSU Acoustic Atlas recordings and the Maine
black bear huff are included as their original MP3 files. Three Yle
Archives clips are excerpts with short boundary fades. The six PLOS clips
are single calls at their original speed. Excerpts, format changes, and
gain adjustments are listed per file in the catalog.

NonCommercial recordings retain their individual Creative Commons terms.
The MSU Acoustic Atlas recording also has a NoDerivatives restriction and
is distributed as the original file. The Slovenia map point is a country
reference because iNaturalist obscures the recording site.
