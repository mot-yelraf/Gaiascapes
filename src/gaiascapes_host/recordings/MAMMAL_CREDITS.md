## Curation limits

**PoC listening feedback (2026-09-12):** the user reports that the elephant
recording (`freesound-818986.mp3`) sounds like other wildlife, such as frogs or
crickets, rather than a usable elephant call. It remains in this proof of
concept to preserve the demonstrated flow, but is **not an approved elephant
example** and needs replacement before a finished collection is released.
The Elephant source is disabled by default.

Identification follows the recordists. Fox, elephant, and howler monkey remain
broad labels because the source does not establish an exact species. The tiger
is captive at Louisville Zoo; the lion was recorded at Ukutula Game Reserve,
with managed/captive status unresolved. These are shown at their recording
sites, never relocated to a species’ native range. Other source context is
retained in the catalog and shown in the status information.

All files passed a full AudioToolbox decode and non-silent signal checks.
A few isolated full-scale samples occur in the macaque and Belize recordings;
existing playback normalization applies. This is a source-metadata and technical
curation pass, not an independent listening or zoological identification review.
The elephant clip includes insects and birds, with a rumble described around
five seconds. Mountain lion, wolf, and an India-specific elephant recording
remain outside this starter pack until reusable, located material is verified.

Published Freesound map centers are approximate locations. The tiger source
names Louisville Zoo but has no geotag; its approximate site uses the
[zoo’s official event calendar coordinates](https://louisvillezoo.org/events/category/events/2026-06/?outlook-ical=1).
